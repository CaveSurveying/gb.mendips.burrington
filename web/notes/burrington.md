The caves of Burrington Combe, built from the Cave Registry's
[Burrington project](http://cave-registry.org.uk/svn/Burrington/): the data behind the
University of Bristol Spelaeological Society's
[Burrington Area Survey](https://ubss.org.uk/research/cave_surveys/burrington/), released
under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA. Each cave below has a page of its own.

## What's here

- **Drawn plans** (the 2D plan in the 3D view): Read's Cavern and Tween Twins Hole from their
  Therion drawings; Goatchurch Cavern's entrance series from its 2012 resurvey; Pierre's Pot,
  Drunkard's Hole and Bos Swallet from the UBSS's printed surveys, fitted to the data.
- **Passage walls**: Read's, Tween Twins, Goatchurch, and Rod's Pot and Bath Swallet.
- **Names** in the route planner: from the drawings, the printed surveys and the surveyors'
  notes, where there are any.

## What isn't

Some surveys here are older or partial, so a cave may be bigger than it looks:

- **Lionel's Hole**: only about 0.3 km of its maze is in the data.
- **Goatchurch Cavern**: the 2006 survey, with a drawn plan of the entrance series only.
- **Goon's Hole** is a point on the map, with no survey.

Each cave's own page says more. These views are for interest and trip planning alongside a
proper survey and guidebook, not instead of them.
