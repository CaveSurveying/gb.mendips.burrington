The survey here is the UBSS's 2006 survey of Goatchurch Cavern (Graham Mullan, Hannah
Sargent, Andrew Brown, Gina Moseley and Chris Binding): 1.4 km of legs, in the Cave
Registry's [Burrington project](http://cave-registry.org.uk/svn/Burrington/Goatchurch/).

## What's missing

- **The drawn plan covers the entrance series only.** It's the 2012 DistoX resurvey (Stuart
  Alldred, Simon Hadfield, Sarah Rae and others), which stopped after three trips. It has no
  grid position of its own, so it's placed here where its legs lie closest to the 2006
  survey's: expect a metre or two out in places.
- **No passage names**: the 2006 survey's trips are named (Coal, Bloody, Maze, Boulder Maze
  and more) but don't say which passage each one covers, so the route planner only knows the
  entrances.
- **The walls** come from the passage widths and heights measured at each station: a
  rough shape, not a drawing. A few legs whose notes don't fit have none.

## A fuller survey

Goatchurch's full printed surveys aren't openly licensed. The Mendip Caving Group's 1960 and
Mike Rennie's 1963 plans are on the
[MCG's survey page](https://mcg.eastus.cloudapp.azure.com/oldws/sections/caving/cavesurveys.html)
for personal use, and the
[Mendip Cave Registry](https://www.mcra.org.uk/registry/survey_details.php?serial=208) lists
others. Take one, and a guidebook, underground.
