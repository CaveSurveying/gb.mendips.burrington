Lionel's Hole is a maze of about 1 km, but only **about 0.3 km** of it is in the survey data
here: David Cundy's survey in the Cave Registry's
[Burrington project](http://cave-registry.org.uk/svn/Burrington/Lionels/). Most of the cave
isn't shown, and a route found here is not the only way, or necessarily the best.

## What's here

The data's branches, named from the surveyor's notes: the entrance rift and Boulder Chamber,
the Letterbox, the Traverse, Junction Chamber, West Low Level and the far streamway.

## A fuller survey

No fuller survey data is openly published. The
[Mendip Cave Registry's references for Lionel's Hole](https://bib.mcra.org.uk/refs/viewer?cave=Lion)
list the published surveys and descriptions. It's an easy cave to get lost in: take a full
survey and a guidebook.
